## Next mechanism: reduce private LP reoptimization work

Do not add more heuristic time, a deeper dive or name-specific rules first.
The current cold private LP consumes the entire slice on both lotsize screen
runs; danoint can retain a seven-fractional-variable point but makes only one
successful private LP step. This is a concrete bottleneck to measure.

1. Retain a bounded replay of the actual private model, compatible canonical
   structure/bounds/objective, and input basis. Separate construction,
   factorization, Phase I, pricing and refinement work. Never export its dual
   bound as a tree bound.
2. Test one compatible warm-reoptimization mechanism. The present sparse LP
   accepts warm bases through a dual-simplex path; changing objective can break
   dual feasibility even when a basis remains primal feasible. Verify the
   current model's structure and primal/dual feasibility before choosing an
   appropriate reoptimization path. Do not blindly attach a root or auxiliary
   projection basis to a different model.
3. Keep the same total pump/dive/repair slices and tolerance checks. Require
   successful replay, MAX/MIN/negative/general-integer tests, alternate-branch
   lifecycle tests and unchanged disabled semantics before repeated measurements.
4. Protect eil33-2/rout coverage, blend2 repair, p0201 completion, gt2 quality and
   pk1 variability; keep all 27 wave outcomes. Record cases where the new path
   never runs, and do not attribute their timing-driven differences to it.

A low fixed-target distance does not ensure an integer completion exists near
that target. If reoptimization work is not the limiting mechanism on a case,
report that result and reassess; do not keep increasing budgets until it looks
successful. Improving the still-large proof gaps is a separate bounds/branching
experiment after first-feasible work, not something a distance heuristic proves.
