## Boundaries to preserve

- The [iteration 37 report](../02-software/solver/reports/milp-iteration-37/REPORT.md) records why Gomory cuts were demoted from the recommended profile. A stronger relaxation alone is not enough to reverse that decision.
- The [industrial-case record](../02-software/solver/docs/INDUSTRIAL_CASES.md) distinguishes literature MARCO/TRNSPORT examples from synthetic production data. The collected refinery papers and MRPL annual report do not turn those examples into validated MRPL plant models.
- The [GPU evaluation](../02-software/solver/reports/kaggle-gpu-03/REPORT.md) concerns box-constrained convex QP. cuPDLP and general ADMM papers are directions to investigate, not evidence of current general LP/MILP GPU support.
- QPLIB includes convex, nonconvex and integer classes. The documentation and paper are separate entries because classification and parsing both matter.
- The full refinery scheduling formulations include nonlinear quality relationships. LP/MILP subproblems can be studied now; complete MINLP support remains an extension.
- External solver papers are mathematical/architectural references. The PS's from-scratch rule remains in force, and benchmark/reference solvers must stay separate from the shipped core.

## Selection and use

The first pass inspected the supplied PS, the existing solver methods and experiment reports, and the reference repository's research indexes. Sources were then selected from primary author, publisher, university, benchmark and official documentation sites. Each retained source has a specific relevance sentence; sources were not added merely to match the reference repository's paper count.

Use the topic README to browse, the `.source.md` note to check version and scope, and the PDF for detailed reading. Citation-only items are explicitly marked. [CURATION.md](CURATION.md) records the collection checks and access limitations.
